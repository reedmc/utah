Slot canyon creek hike, red canyon drive, Zion Guru gear-up
The most drive-heavy day of the trip — and it earned it. Kanarra is nowhere near Orderville: ~65 miles / ~1.5 hrs each way via UT-14 through Cedar Canyon, up by Cedar City. Leaving at 7:00 AM was the right call.

Day 3 · Tuesday, August 11
Zion / The Narrows / E-bike Day
Into the canyon early, river hiking all morning
Best logistics decision of the trip. The e-bikes meant no shuttle line, no waiting, and a start early enough to have the canyon mostly to ourselves.
Very Early Morning
Picked up e-bikes at Zion Guru and rode into the park
  • Grab-and-go — sizing and paperwork were done at Monday's check-in
  • Rode the Pa'rus Trail in to Temple of Sinawava, locked the bikes at the rack
  • Walked the Riverside Walk (1 mile) to the Narrows entry
No shuttle needed ~15–20 min ride in
Morning
The Narrows — bottom-up Trip highlight
  • Waded upstream as far as conditions and energy allowed, then turned around
  • Canyon walls kept it shaded through the morning — a completely different experience than the canyon floor in the afternoon
  • The rented river shoes, neoprene socks, and poles made a real difference on the slick rock
River hiking Shaded until late morning ~3–4 hrs
Afternoon
Returned the bikes; back to Orderville
  • No second activity — the Narrows was the whole day, and that was plenty
~40 min drive

What We Came Home With
  • Kanarra Falls slot canyon, ladders and all
  • The Narrows, reached by e-bike with no shuttle line
  • A dusty 5-hour UTV run to the Grand Canyon North Rim
  • Dinosaur tracks and Belly of the Dragon on a whim
  • Bryce from below (Queen's Garden/Navajo) and from above (Bristlecone, Inspiration Point)
  • A last Zion morning — Canyon Overlook, Emerald Pools, Weeping Rock
  • The Bellagio fountains and one very good night walking the Strip
What Worked
  • E-bikes in Zion — skipping the shuttle entirely was the single best logistics call
  • One outfitter for everything — Zion Guru handled Narrows gear and bikes in one appointment
  • Orderville as a base — ~40 min to Zion, ~1 hr to Bryce, 15 min to Kanab, kitchen at the VRBO
  • Early starts — every hike was done before the worst of the afternoon heat
  • Front-loading the hard days — Kanarra → Narrows → UTV → Bryce kept the effort in the fresh half of the week
  • Saying no — the days we cut a second activity were the days we enjoyed most
Notes for Next Time
  • Kanarra Falls really is ~1.5 hrs each way from Orderville — pair it with Kolob or skip it
  • Bryce's southern end (Rainbow Point / Bristlecone) is worth the drive and much cooler
  • Kanarra was the only permit we needed all week
  • Still on the list: Valley of Fire, Coral Pink Sand Dunes, Sand Hollow, Fairyland Loop

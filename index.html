Day 2 · Monday
Kanarra Falls + Kolob Canyons
Slot canyon creek hike, red canyon drive, Zion Guru gear-up
Most drive-heavy day of the trip (~3 hrs round trip) — start early. Kanarra is NOT near Orderville: it's ~65 miles / 1 hr 20 min–1.5 hr each way via UT-14 through Cedar Canyon, up by Cedar City. Reserve the paid, daily-capped permit in advance at kanarrafalls.com, and check the flash flood forecast morning-of before entering the canyon.
Early Morning · 7:00–8:30 AM MT
Drive to Kanarra Falls trailhead (~1.5 hrs from Orderville)

  • Depart Orderville by ~7:00 AM to beat the heat and secure parking
  • Wear water shoes; prepare for multiple creek crossings
  • Permit required — have your kanarrafalls.com reservation ready

Day 3 · Tuesday
Zion / The Narrows / E-bike Day
Into the canyon early, river hiking, afternoon flex
Start early. The Narrows gets crowded fast and canyon floor temps reach 90–105°F by midday. E-bikes let you skip the shuttle entirely.
Very Early Morning
Pick up e-bikes at Zion Guru (pickup time confirmed at yesterday's 5–7 PM check-in)
  • Sizing, waivers, and payment were handled yesterday — quick grab-and-go
  • Ride the Pa'rus Trail into the park (~2.5 miles to Temple of Sinawava shuttle stop)
  • Lock bikes at the rack; walk the Riverside Walk (1 mile) to the Narrows entry
Water shoes or canyoneering sandals for Narrows Trekking poles available from outfitters ~15–20 min bike ride in
Morning · 4:30–8:30 AM MT
The Narrows — Bottom-Up
  • Enter from the Riverside Walk; wade upstream as far as conditions and energy allow
  • First ~1 mile is wade-free; narrows tighten dramatically past Orderville Canyon junction
  • Turn around when you feel like it — no set endpoint required
  • Flash flood risk: check NPS forecast at zionpark.com before entering
River hiking — expect to be wet Canyon walls provide shade until ~10 AM ~3–4 hrs (flexible turnaround)
9:00 AM–2:30 PM MT — TBD based on energy

  • Emerald Pools — Lower Pool is 1.2 mi RT, easy; Upper Pool is 3 mi RT, moderate; waterfalls and hanging gardens (~1–2.5 hrs)

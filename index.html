🏜️ Vegas to Southern Utah

6-Day Family Road Trip Itinerary

Trip Overview

Duration: 6 days / 5 nights
Group: 2 adults, 2 adult sons
Base: Orderville, Utah
Pace: Balanced adventure & downtime

Must-Have Experiences

  • Zion National Park — Full-day priority
  • Bryce Canyon National Park — Full-day priority
  • Hiking & nature — Core activity for all
  • Scenic drives & overlooks — Essential to the region
  • Water activities — Swimming & river experiences
  • Las Vegas experience — Arrival day lobby/casino walk
Day 1: Sunday — Arrival Day

Flight & Arrival

Fly into Las Vegas. Land in early afternoon PT. Pick up rental car and begin your southwest adventure.

Afternoon Activity

Las Vegas Strip walking tour — explore iconic casino lobbies, people watch, and soak in the Vegas energy. Low-key, no gambling focus.

Evening Drive

Drive to Orderville, Utah (~2.5 hours via I-15 N and US-89). Arrive at your base lodging in the evening. Arrange key access in advance.

Lodging: AirBnB in Orderville with full kitchen. Check-in at 4:00 PM.
Day 2: Monday — Kanarra Falls & Kolob Canyons

Morning Hike

Kanarra Falls hike (3.7 miles, moderate) — slot canyon with creek crossings and waterfalls. Wear water shoes. Check flash flood forecast before departure.

Afternoon Scenic Drive

Kolob Canyons scenic loop (15 minutes away). Optional: Timber Creek Overlook Trail for more views.

Late Afternoon Stops in Springdale

  • Zion Guru: Narrows gear fitting & pickup (shoes, neoprene socks, walking poles). 2:00–7:30 PM available.
  • Greater Zion E-Bikes: Sizing, paperwork, and set early-bird pickup time for tomorrow. Coupon available for one free bike.
Permit Required: Kanarra Falls ($15/person). Book well in advance—August dates fill up fast. 150-person daily cap.
Day 3: Tuesday — Zion / The Narrows / E-Bikes

Early Morning

Grab pre-charged e-bikes as early as 4:00 AM (Early Bird Special). Ride into Zion independently—no shuttle lines.

Morning Hike

The Narrows — enter from the bottom (Riverside Walk trailhead), hike as far as conditions and energy allow. Best done early before heat and crowds peak. You'll be in the Virgin River—this is the water activity for today.

Afternoon (Energy-Dependent)

  • Virgin River swimming
  • Canyon Overlook Trail (moderate scenic hike)
  • Relax and decompress in Springdale
Heat Strategy: August temps on Zion floor reach 90–105°F. Early morning starts are essential. Bring 1–2L water per person minimum.
Day 4: Wednesday — Grand Canyon North Rim UTV Tour

Anchor Activity

North Rim UTV Tour with Expedition Kanab — 5-hour guided experience. 10:00 AM departure from Kanab (15 min from your base).

You can split into two UTVs if preferred, allowing the sons to have their own vehicle. Stunning views, high desert adventure, and a unique perspective on the Grand Canyon's less-visited rim.

Afternoon Optional Activity

Coral Pink Sand Dunes State Park (~30 minutes from Kanab) — great add-on if energy allows. Sand dunes, possibly a four-wheeler or hiking option.

✓ BOOKED
Day 5: Thursday — Bryce Canyon National Park

Pre-Dawn Drive

Drive from Orderville to Bryce (~1 hour). Arrive at Inspiration Point just before sunrise for the best light and smallest crowds.

Morning Hike

Queen's Garden + Navajo Loop Combo (2.9 miles, moderate) — The consensus best single hike in the park. Descend on Queen's Garden from Sunrise Point; climb out via Navajo Loop/Wall Street to Sunset Point.

Sons' Option: Strenuous Add-On

Fairyland Loop (8 miles, strenuous) — If the boys want to push further while parents complete the moderate combo hike.

Midday

Southern Scenic Drive — Enter from the back of the park to avoid crowd flow. Stop at Natural Bridge and Aqua Canyon viewpoints. Picnic lunch at Rainbow Point (highest point in park).

Afternoon Cooldown

Mossy Cave Trail (0.9 miles, easy) — Just outside the park boundary on Route 12. Flat, follows a small stream to a waterfall. Perfect wind-down after a big morning.

Evening

Last night at the Orderville AirBnB. Pack your bags tonight for a smooth checkout tomorrow morning.

Parking Tip: Arrive before 9:00 AM or use the free park shuttle. Sunset Point parking fills fast. Bring 0.5L water per person per hour of hiking. No permits required for day hikes.
Day 6: Friday — Drive Back to Las Vegas

Morning (Short Activity)

Optional quick stop on the drive back (on the road by 10–11 AM):

  • Buckskin Gulch peek
  • Moqui Cave in Kanab
  • Leisurely final checkout

Midday Drive

Drive west toward Las Vegas via St. George.

Afternoon Stop

Sand Hollow State Park, Hurricane, UT — On the I-15 corridor, perfect stop on the way back to Vegas. Cliff jumping and swimming available. Don't linger too long; target Vegas arrival by early evening.

Evening

Check into your Las Vegas hotel. Explore the Strip at your leisure or enjoy dinner on the property.

Lodging: One night at the Aria on the Las Vegas Strip. Enjoy the evening but keep it relaxed—early flight tomorrow.
Day 7: Saturday — Departure

Morning Departure

Check out from hotel and head to the airport. Target security by 10:00 AM for your 11:30 AM flight. Depart hotel ~9:00 AM.

End of Trip

Safe travels home!

Key Logistics & Reminders

  • Transportation: Rental car from Las Vegas airport
  • Base Camp: Orderville AirBnB with full kitchen (ideal location between Zion and Bryce)
  • America the Beautiful Pass: $80/year covers Zion + Bryce + all federal parks
  • Heat Strategy: Early morning starts for all hikes. Hydrate constantly.
  • Permits: Kanarra Falls requires advance booking. No permits needed for day hikes at Bryce or Zion's most popular trails.
  • E-Bikes & Gear: Pre-arranged at Springdale outfitters. Eliminates shuttle wait times in Zion.

Day 2 · Monday
Kanarra Falls + Kolob Canyons
Slot canyon creek hike, red canyon drive, e-bike setup
✓ Permits required — kanarrafalls.com · $15/person. Book well in advance. Check flash flood forecast morning-of before entering the canyon.
Early Morning
Drive to Kanarra Falls trailhead (~40 min from Orderville)
  • Kanarraville, UT — trailhead at town end of 100 West St
  • Wear water shoes; prepare for multiple creek crossings
Permit required — $15/person Water shoes essential Trekking poles helpful
Morning
Kanarra Falls Hike
  • 3.7 miles RT, moderate — slot canyon narrows, waterfalls, ladders
  • Crowds build after 9 AM; early start means the slot canyon to yourselves
  • Upper falls requires a short ladder climb — sons will love it
Midday
Kolob Canyons — Scenic Drive (15 min from Kanarraville)
  • 5-mile paved road ending at Timber Creek Overlook trailhead
  • Massive red canyon walls; far less crowded than Zion main canyon
Zion NP entrance fee applies
Optional add-on
Timber Creek Overlook Trail
  • 1 mile RT, easy — panoramic views of the Kolob peaks
  • Skip if energy is low after Kanarra; the drive alone is worth the stop
After Kolob
Call ahead — Narrows gear availability
  • Zion Adventures (first choice) — call to check gear availability for pickup this afternoon
  • Zion Guru (alternate) — call if Zion Adventures can't confirm
Afternoon
E-bike + Narrows Gear Outfitter Stops — Springdale Do this today
  • Springdale is ~25 min from Kolob Canyons
  • Greater Zion E-Bikes: Handle sizing, waivers, and payment; confirm pick-up time for tomorrow morning (Early Bird Special available as early as 4 AM)
  • Zion Guru: Narrows gear fitting & pickup (shoes, neoprene socks, walking poles). Available 2:00–7:30 PM.
Reserve in advance ~$50–70/bike/day
Late Afternoon
Drive back to Orderville (~40 min)
  • Dinner in Springdale before leaving, or cook at the AirBnB
Day 3 · Tuesday
Zion / The Narrows / E-bike Day
Into the canyon early, river hiking, afternoon flex
Start early. The Narrows gets crowded fast and canyon floor temps reach 90–105°F by midday. E-bikes let you skip the shuttle entirely.
Very Early Morning
Pick up e-bikes in Springdale (Early Bird Special as early as 4:00 AM)
  • Sizing, waivers, and payment were handled yesterday — quick grab-and-go
  • Ride the Pa'rus Trail into the park (~2.5 miles to Temple of Sinawava shuttle stop)
  • Lock bikes at the rack; walk the Riverside Walk (1 mile) to the Narrows entry
Water shoes or canyoneering sandals for Narrows Trekking poles available from outfitters
Morning
The Narrows — Bottom-Up
  • Enter from the Riverside Walk; wade upstream as far as conditions and energy allow
  • First ~1 mile is wade-free; narrows tighten dramatically past Orderville Canyon junction
  • Turn around when you feel like it — no set endpoint required
  • Flash flood risk: check NPS forecast at zionpark.com before entering
River hiking — expect to be wet Canyon walls provide shade until ~10 AM
Afternoon — TBD based on energy
Open slot — pick one
  • Virgin River swimming — wade the Virgin near the Zion Lodge area; shaded, calm, refreshing
  • Canyon Overlook Trail — 1 mile RT, easy-moderate; great canyon views; trailhead just east of Zion–Mt. Carmel Tunnel
  • Emerald Pools — Lower Pool is 1.2 mi RT, easy; Upper Pool is 3 mi RT, moderate; waterfalls and hanging gardens
  • Decompress in Springdale — food, ice cream, gear browse; return bikes by rental deadline
Late Afternoon
Return e-bikes; drive back to Orderville
  • Dinner in Springdale or cook at the AirBnB

Day 5 · Thursday
Bryce Canyon Day
Sunrise, hoodoos, scenic drive, wind-down waterfall
Early start required. Leave Orderville pre-dawn. Arrive Inspiration Point before sunrise. Parking at Sunset Point fills by 8–9 AM.
Pre-Dawn
Inspiration Point — Sunrise
  • Better views and fewer people than Sunrise Point
  • ~10 min walk from parking to rim
Morning
Queen's Garden + Navajo Loop Combo Best hike in park
  • 2.9 miles, ~550 ft elevation change, moderate
  • Descend Queen's Garden from Sunrise Point; climb out Navajo Loop/Wall Street to Sunset Point
  • Shuttle back to Sunrise Point (~5 min) or walk the rim trail (~0.5 mi)
0.5L/person/hour Exposed trail — hat + sunscreen
Sons add-on (optional)
Fairyland Loop
  • 8 miles, strenuous — break off while parents do Queen's Garden/Navajo
  • Less crowded; starts at Fairyland Point (north of visitor center)
  • Plan ~4–5 hrs; meet back at Sunset Point lot
Late Morning through Afternoon
Southern Scenic Drive
  • Enter from Rainbow Point end (southernmost); drive north — avoids traffic flow
  • Stops: Natural Bridge, Agua Canyon overlook, Yovimpa Point
  • Picnic lunch at Rainbow Point (~8,000 ft — cooler than canyon floor)
~18 miles one way
Afternoon
Mossy Cave Trail (wind-down)
  • 0.9 miles, easy — flat trail follows a stream to a small waterfall and mossy alcove
  • Located just outside the park boundary on UT-12
  • Feet in the water is a legit option here
Stream access
Late Afternoon
Drive back to Orderville (~1 hr)
  • Last night at the AirBnB — pack tonight so Day 6 checkout is smooth
Day 6 · Friday
Drive-to-Vegas Day
Morning activity, Sand Hollow swim, Strip hotel
Morning
Last Kanab/Orderville Morning Activity
Open slot — pick one (keep it short; on the road by 10–11 AM)
  • Buckskin Gulch / Wire Pass trailhead — quick peek into one of the world's longest slot canyons; even a 1-mile in-and-out is spectacular; ~45 min from Orderville
  • Moqui Cave (Kanab) — quirky roadside cave/museum with Native American artifacts and dinosaur tracks; low-key, air-conditioned, 30 min
  • Kanab morning coffee + stroll — relax, hit Rocking V Café or the Kanab Creamery, check out the old Western movie sets
  • Skip morning activity — leisurely checkout, load the car, head west early; more time at Sand Hollow or Vegas
Late Morning · Check out by 11 AM · Drive west on US-89 → I-15

Sand Hollow State Park — Hurricane, UT Don't skip
  • Red rock reservoir with cliff jumping, swimming, and a sandy beach
  • Right on the I-15 corridor — natural stop on the way to Vegas
  • Entry ~$20/vehicle; no reservations needed
  • Don't linger past mid-afternoon — need Vegas arrival by early evening
Swimming + cliff jumping Bring towels + change of clothes
